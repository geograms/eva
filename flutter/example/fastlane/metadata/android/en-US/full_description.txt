Eva is a fully offline AI assistant for Android. The language model, speech
recognition, text-to-speech, document search, maps, music and Wikipedia all run
on the phone. No account, no cloud, nothing leaves the device. Internet is only
used for optional on-demand downloads (a model, a Wikipedia edition, a map tile,
a radio stream) — never for your data.

Features:

* Chat with selectable on-device models (downloaded on demand or sideloaded).
  Answers are grounded in your documents, the offline Wikipedia, and the model's
  own knowledge, with citations you can open at the passage.
* Use Eva as the phone's digital assistant; talk to it with offline speech-to-text
  and spoken replies. Attach a photo and ask about it with a vision model.
* Offline maps & navigation: show places on a map with your live GPS dot, draw a
  walking route, and cache areas for offline use.
* A full offline music player: browse by folder (grouped into genres by the
  model), artist and favourites; queue, shuffle, save playlists; background
  playback with lock-screen controls.
* An e-reader: read the offline Wikipedia (search, random, recently viewed),
  browse your documents grouped into categories by the model, and resume PDFs at
  the last page.
* A photo gallery you can search by on-device caption and your own tags, with
  GPS-located photos shown on the map.
* Online radio, and timed reminders that fire as notifications.
* A guided first-run setup to choose what to download per topic, with progress
  and resumable downloads; one storage folder (e.g. an SD card) for all data.

Eva runs on the Cactus inference engine and reads the offline Wikipedia from a
Kiwix ZIM via libzim.
