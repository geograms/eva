First-run setup wizard: choose which models, the document-search model, and
which offline Wikipedia editions to download per topic; pick a storage folder
(reusing anything already on it); per-item and overall download progress; and
downloads that resume if interrupted.

Also includes: offline maps & navigation, a full music player with playlists and
background/lock-screen playback, the Wikipedia reader with search-as-you-type,
documents and music grouped into categories by the model, a photo gallery with
tags and GPS, online radio, and timed reminders.
