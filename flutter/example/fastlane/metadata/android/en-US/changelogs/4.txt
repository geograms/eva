In-app updates: a new "App updates" panel (Settings) shows your installed and
available version and can download and install new releases for you. With
"Automatic updates" on, Eva downloads the new version in the background and
opens the installer — you just confirm the final step. You can also point it at
a different update source if needed.

Documents: image-only / scanned PDFs (no selectable text) are no longer skipped
— they're now listed in their folder and findable by their title, author and
filename. Added support for FictionBook (.fb2) and HTML (.htm/.html) files.
