Document discovery now runs as a true background service: scanning the phone
for PDFs and text files keeps going when you switch apps or the screen is off,
so large libraries (thousands of files) are found completely instead of stopping
when you leave the screen. Newly found files are queued for indexing as the scan
proceeds.

The Indexer panel gains a "Rescan for files" button on Documents, Music and
Photos to pick up newly-added files on demand, and now shows the document
discovery phase ("Scanning · N files") separately from indexing.

Settings: Indexer and Storage moved to the top of the menu so they're easy to
reach.
